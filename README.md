# Eco Genie – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Eco Genie is a Dubai-based professional water tank cleaning and disinfection company serving residential, commercial, and industrial properties. Its services include tank inspection, deep cleaning, sediment removal, disinfection, and final quality inspection.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Eco Genie as a professional water hygiene specialist, with clearer service information, easier pricing comparisons, and stronger customer confidence through consistent business messaging.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Regulatory Credibility
- Improved the presentation of the company's hygiene and compliance-related information.
- Addressed the limited visibility of supporting regulatory credentials.
- Avoided presenting unverified certifications or approvals as established facts.
- Created a clearer foundation for displaying genuine compliance documentation.

### 2. Simplified Service and Pricing Selection
- Improved the organisation of residential, commercial, and industrial cleaning services.
- Presented the four pricing options more clearly:
  - Basic Care: AED 399
  - Standard Care: AED 599
  - Premium Care: AED 899
  - Commercial Care: Custom quotation
- Made package selection and quotation enquiries easier to understand.

### 3. Improved Brand and Content Consistency
- Addressed references to unrelated business names, including Golden Spotless.
- Identified repetitive blog titles and inconsistent content presentation as issues to correct.
- Focused the homepage messaging consistently on Eco Genie's water tank cleaning and disinfection services.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more professional presentation focused on water hygiene and property maintenance.
- **Layout:** Improved the organisation of service categories, pricing packages, and important business information.
- **User Experience:** Made it easier for visitors to understand available services, compare packages, and find enquiry options.
- **Content Consistency:** Focused messaging on Eco Genie and its core water tank cleaning services.
- **Trust Presentation:** Prioritised genuine business credentials and supporting compliance information without introducing unsupported claims.
- **Conversion Focus:** Strengthened the presentation of telephone, WhatsApp, and quotation enquiry pathways.

The redesigned homepage gives Eco Genie a clearer commercial identity, simplifies the service selection process, and provides a more consistent platform for generating residential and commercial water tank cleaning enquiries across Dubai.
